## Construction

Same rules that v4.1/v4.2 established and that the filing validated:

- **SpaceX** — the filed 36,938,300 shares ÷ shares-out. Not the disclosed percentage; the share
  count is the harder number and the percentage is the check on it.
- **Top-9** — disclosed 8/31 %-of-gross, taken at face value.
- **Tail (20 names)** — BUY-AND-HOLD off v4.2's share book, one uniform **−5.49%** haircut to fit the
  forced residual (tail = 24.94% of gross). Not re-imposed weights, which would sell winners and buy
  losers — the rule the 6/30 NPORT confirmed when it showed TSLA's absolute share count moved only
  −0.2% across a month.
- **MRNA** stays where the back-solve put it, carried through the haircut: 0.02226 sh/BPTIX.

The −5.49% haircut is the tail absorbing two things it cannot separate: SpaceX taking a larger share
of gross on price alone, and the fund de-levering 1.100 → 1.092. Neither is a reason to re-weight
the tail by hand.

## Backtest — the crossover is exactly where it should be

Level error, every session 7/31 → 9/9, v4.2 (anchor 7/31) against v5 (anchor 8/31):

| window | v4.2 RMS | v5 RMS | winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aug 1st half (7/31–8/14) | **0.207** | 0.552 | v4.2 |
| Aug 2nd half (8/17–8/31) | **0.167** | 0.207 | v4.2 |
| **September (9/1–9/9)** | 0.213 | **0.066** | **v5, by 3.2×** |
| full window (n=28) | 0.194 | 0.370 | v4.2 |

**The crossover lands on 8/27.** From that day v5 wins every session except 9/2.

This is precisely the pattern a monthly re-anchor should produce: each basket is most accurate near
its own anchor and decays away from it. v4.2 leads the full window only because 22 of its 28 days sit
inside *its* anchor period. Had the ordering been anything else — v5 better in early August, or v4.2
still better in September — something would have been wrong with the construction.

Like-for-like, each basket over the first 6 sessions after **its own** anchor:

| | n | RMS | bias |
|---|---|---|---|
| v4.2, 8/3–8/10 | 6 | 0.257 | +0.196 |
| **v5, 9/1–9/9** | 6 | **0.066** | −0.044 |

v5's out-of-sample start is ~4× cleaner than v4.2's was, on both RMS and bias. **Caveat: n=6.**
Provisional until another two weeks are in.
