## Carry: why every static basket "drifts", and how much

v6's raw error went +0.02 → +0.62 → +0.45 over 10/12–11/13. Three things a static per-BPTIX
basket cannot see, each now modelled in `daily_nav_log.py` and taken out of the score:

1. **Expenses — 1.65%/yr** (9/30 fact sheet: 1.04% operating + 0.61% interest, as of
   4/30/2026). The NAV is struck net of the daily accrual; the basket's borrow line is not.
   ≈ **$0.0135/BPTIX per calendar day**, ~$0.41/month. v6's error slope matched it.
2. **Dividends on held stocks.** The fund books the receivable on the ex-date; the basket
   only sees the price drop. First case in-window: 11/13, MSCI $2.05 + SCHW $0.32 ≈
   $0.09/BPTIX. Recorded per day as `ex_divs` in `ENTRIES`.
3. **Redemptions.** Fund-level SpaceX and borrowings stay fixed while shares-out falls
   (55.86M at 10/9 → 55.21M at 11/13), so **SpaceX per BPTIX rises** (0.6613 → 0.6690) and the
   cash paid out comes from selling the **public names pro-rata**. Rule of thumb: for each 1%
   fall in shares-out, SpaceX and borrow per BPTIX rise 1% and every public line falls
   ~0.27% (= 1 − 1/(L − w_spx) with public ≈ 0.79 × NAV); since 11/2 that is ×0.9988.
   Paying with extra borrowing instead fits worse (fee-adjusted
   RMS 0.066 vs 0.045). **The 11/2 holdings confirm it: SpaceX 30.39% predicted, 30.4%
   disclosed; static v6 said 30.17%.** Shares-out comes from the exact AUM prints and is
   carried over gaps — no look-ahead.

v6, 10/12–11/13 (24 sessions): raw RMS 0.31 → net of 1+2: 0.07 → net of 1+2+3: **0.05**.
